CLI arguments for `wearables-display`.

The tool takes no user-facing flags — it is a `Stop` lifecycle hook driven entirely by the HookEvent piped to stdin. `event_json` is not a CLI flag: it is populated from stdin in [`WearablesDisplayTool::prepare`] (cell-side) so the raw event travels across the privsep boundary inside the serialized args, since the trusted worker has no stdin of its own.

Usage: wearables-display

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')
