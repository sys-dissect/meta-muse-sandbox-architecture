Describe the user's Muse subscription, usage, and available plans

Usage: subscription-status <COMMAND>

Commands:
  status    Describe the current subscription, usage, and credit availability
  plans     Describe the current tier and available subscription plans and prices
  overview  Describe both current usage and available subscription plans
  help      Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help
