Track shipments through Hatch's managed AfterShip connector

Usage: aftership [OPTIONS] <COMMAND>

Commands:
  status          Check connector availability. --verify performs a lightweight AfterShip probe
  detect-courier  Detect possible couriers for a tracking number
  tracking        Read an existing tracking record
  register        Register a tracking number with AfterShip

Options:
      --compact  Print compact JSON
  -h, --help     Print help
