Share, remove, or check artifact deployment status.

Usage: share <COMMAND>

Commands:
  remove           Take down a legacy shared artifact (un-share only)
  share-space      Share a published space
  unshare-space    Un-share a published space
  fetch-space-url  Check if a space is currently shared
  export-html      Export a built static space as one standalone .html file under workspace/your_files/<slug>/, with its asset references inlined as data: URIs
  help             Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help
